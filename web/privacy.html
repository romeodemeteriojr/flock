Privacy

Flock is the volunteer roster our church uses to plan who serves when. This page says what it keeps about you and why.

What Flock keeps

  • Your name and email address, added by a scheduler at the church.
  • What you can do (for example Door or Sound), when you are scheduled, whether you accepted, your days off and how often you want to serve.
  • If you set a password: a scrambled form of it. Nobody can read your password, not even the schedulers.
  • If you sign in with Google: an id number Google gives Flock for your account, so it can recognise you next time.
  • If you turn on notifications: the address your phone or browser gave Flock for sending them, for each device you turned them on.

Your phone or browser keeps a copy of Flock's own files (its pages, pictures and lettering, nothing about you) so it opens quickly. Your schedule and everything else about you always comes fresh from the church's Flock.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on, one device at a time. Then Flock tells you when you are asked to serve or when a service you are on moves or is cancelled (schedulers also hear when someone cannot serve). They travel through the push service of your phone or browser (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft). The message is encrypted so only your device can read it; the push service sees only that a message arrived. Turning notifications off, logging out on that device, or a scheduler resetting your login removes the device from Flock.

Signing in with Google

Flock asks Google only for your email address and that id number. It does not see your name, photo, contacts, calendar, files or anything else in your Google account, and it does not keep any access to your Google account after you sign in. Your email is used once, to match you to the person a scheduler added.

Who can see it

  • The church's schedulers see everyone's details, so they can plan the roster.
  • Other volunteers see names on the schedule, not emails.
  • Nobody else. Flock has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your details are never sold or shared.

Cookies

Flock sets one cookie to keep you logged in until 14 days after you last used it, and a short-lived one while you sign in with Google. There are no other cookies. If you dismiss the invitation to turn on notifications, your phone remembers that, only on that phone.

Where it is kept

On the church's own Flock server, sent over HTTPS only. Flock does not send emails or texts; the only messages it sends are the notifications you turned on.

Changing or removing your details

Ask a scheduler at the church. They can correct your details, reset your login, remove your email and deactivate you. Past rosters keep your name, so the church's history stays correct.
